type: Added
title: Snow is an engine feature
---

`ge::snow` (target `ge::snow`) lays a layer of snow over any ground and remembers what crossed it: an analytic cover that costs no memory until something presses into it, sparse fixed-point tiles of columns where something has, one `sample` query for the physics, the picture and the sound, and `press` for any contact (a capsule, a disc, a box or an ellipse; a load or a sink) that packs the snow with its mass kept to the unit and throws part of it aside as a berm. A typical ski, boot, tyre, snowmobile track and fallen body come with their numbers; `snow::save` writes a layer for a save game or a late joiner, and `ge snow show` and `ge snow column` read one without the game. New labs `snow-press` and `snow-field`, and a `snow` consumer skill.
