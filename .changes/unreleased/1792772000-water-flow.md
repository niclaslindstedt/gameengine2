title: Flowing water
---

`water::Flow` (`engine/water/flow.hpp`) simulates water on a grid over a bed: a dam broken, a basin emptying through a breach, a flood finding its way down a valley, a river over a cliff as a falling sheet. Springs, drains and open edges; the water is counted in whole quanta, so none is made or lost, and the same bits come out on any number of threads. Its level of detail merges the cells 2x2 and 4x4 away from every actor, holding the water and the walls, and lets a far, settled region sleep. `add_flow` lays a region in a water world, so `sample` reads its surface and current. The `flow`, `water-lod` and `water-cost` labs.
