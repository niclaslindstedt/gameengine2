type: Added
title: A body walks a room level on the mover's own collision
---

A room level is now a world a character moves through: `plugins/rooms/clip.hpp` hands the mover the level's prisms as the flat geometry they are stored as (floors, walls with their openings, frames and solids, every relief left off) and its caves as the rock they grow into, and `engine/move/mesh_world.hpp` answers the mover's swept-box trace against any triangle mesh, exact at edges and corners and bucketed in a grid. `plugins/rooms/walk.hpp` plans a tour of every room by its doorways, routed inside each room by the mover's own sweeps, and walks it with the walker preset; the `rooms` lab draws the walk over the plan and the rooms through the walker's eyes. The labs' model view now cuts a triangle at the near plane instead of dropping it, so a big floor or ceiling draws with the eye standing in the room.
