type: Added
title: The engine's own level format, rooms grown from their seeds
---

`plugins/rooms` is a level format of its own: rooms as a footprint between a floor and a ceiling, openings between them with frames, solids standing in them, and materials naming a texture reference and a relief, in a few bytes a room (`.gelv`, with a `.gelv.txt` text form an agent writes and reads). Each room carries a seed its surfaces grow from at load, the same bits on every machine and thread count: flat walls stay a few quads, and eight reliefs grow (rock, crag, fieldstone, ashlar, tiles, planks, corrugated sheet, dented plate) on walls, floors and ceilings; a cave is hollowed into rock by a seeded field and surface nets, its mouths cut to the doorway. The `rooms` lab pictures a level from above, cut away, room by room, relief by relief, a cave from four seeds and from inside, with its bytes, triangles and build times.
