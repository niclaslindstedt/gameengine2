title: Camera lenses for Fall Line's snowmobile, helicopter and paramotor
---

Each of the free ride's machines is seen through a camera ladder of its own: a worn lens at the rider's eyes (on the snowmobile's boards, the helicopter's skid, in the paramotor's harness), a chase that banks with the machine in the air, a far lens, and a high lens or a look down the long fall under a craft. Boarding a machine or leaving it hands the picture over in a one-second blend on the matching rung, never a cut. A boom can now bank with its subject in the air by its own share (`BoomRow::bank_air`). The `camera-machines` lab pictures every machine's rungs at the moments of the bot's outings and every hand-over.
