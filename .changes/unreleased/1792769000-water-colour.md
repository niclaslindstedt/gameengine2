title: The colour of water from what is in it
---

`water::optics` (`engine/water/optics.hpp`) derives a body of water's colour from its constituents (pure water, dissolved organic matter, chlorophyll, sediment, bubbles, the bed), spectrally: the absorption and scattering per band, the colour from above over the bed at any depth, the veil under water and the visibility. Eleven named looks, from a brown forest lake to tropical shallows, a glacial lake, a muddy river, a pool, slime and lava; a body takes its look's optics when it is laid, or a game states its own water. The `water-colour` lab draws every look from above, along the shore and from below.
