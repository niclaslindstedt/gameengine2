---
type: Added
title: The wind as an engine module
---

`ge::wind` (`engine/wind`, published as `ge::wind`) makes the wind a pure function of place and time over a `wind::Field`: a mean and bearing at 10 m, gusts and a veer as hashed sines (`lay_gusts` lays a default set for a turbulence intensity), the log-law profile over a ground's roughness, a baked shelter grid that keeps and turns the wind, a world's own rule on bearings, and local sources (a jet such as a rotor's downwash, a whirl, a blast) added as closed forms. The mountain's wind is now one field of it (`mountain::air::field_of`) with its bits unchanged, and the `wind` lab charts the gusts, the profile, a sheltered plan and the sources.
