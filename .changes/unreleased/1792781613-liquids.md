type: Added
title: Liquids: lava and slime
---

Bodies of water take a liquid: viscosity, yield stress, tension and harm (`water/liquid.hpp`, `water/harm.hpp`). The wind's waves and drift, the swimmer and the damage all read it, and there are named rows for two lavas and five slimes (`water/liquids.hpp`). Lava and slime are drawn by a new pass (`render/liquid.hpp`), and `FrameView::adapt_cd` keys the eye to bright emitters.
