title: Network pressure tests and ge net stress
---

`engine/net/stress.hpp` pushes the network until it breaks, on virtual time. Ramps raise loss, bursts, latency, jitter or lost bandwidth until a traffic mix breaks a bound, and bisect to the breaking point. Eight chaos scripts (a flap, a partition, a host lost or restarted, a rebound port, skewed clocks, a loss burst, a bandwidth collapse) check that every client ends reconnected or cleanly timed out. A soak holds memory flat over an hour of 32 clients, and a fuzzer puts a million mutated datagrams beside an honest session. `ge net stress` runs them, and the `net-stress`, `net-chaos` and `net-fuzz` labs chart them.
