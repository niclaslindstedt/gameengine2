---
type: Added
title: Maps in the classic BSP families read into one view
---

`plugins/brush` reads maps of the classic BSP families (version 29 and its larger-limits variant BSP2, version 38 and version 46) into one view whatever the family: surfaces as triangles with their texture and lightmap coordinates, the textures, the lightmaps, the node tree with its leaves and clusters, the decompressed visibility between clusters, the models and the entity lump parsed into key/value blocks; version 46's curved patches are tessellated at a chosen level and a `.lit` file drops coloured light into a version 29 map. Every lump and index is checked as it is read, so a damaged or hostile file is refused with the lump, the record and what to do next. Fixture writers make a small map of every family from a few rooms and lights, and the `bsp` lab pictures each one (from above, from inside, its lightmaps, what each cluster sees, the patch tessellated) with its size and read time.
