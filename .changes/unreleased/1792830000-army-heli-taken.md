title: An army helicopter can be taken or rammed down in Fall Line
---

On a free ride an army helicopter set down on the snow can be taken: a skier at its pilot's door who presses the machine key takes it over as the free ride's own helicopter, its crew left on the ground, and the theft raises the wanted level to at least four stars. Flown into a hunting, leaving or landing army machine (rotor disc into rotor disc, or hull into hull), the free ride's helicopter crashes and the army's falls spinning onto the snow, lies there as a wreck and is cleared two minutes later; downing one raises the wanted level to at least three stars. The `army-flight` lab's new `steal` and `ram` scenarios table and picture both.
