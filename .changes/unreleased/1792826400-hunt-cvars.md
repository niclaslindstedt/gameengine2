title: The hunt's server cvars in Fall Line
---

Fall Line's hunt can be set from a cfg or the console: `sv_lethal` (the army shoots to kill, or chases the skier down and arrests him), `sv_armyfire` (the army's guns fire at all), `sv_helis` and `sv_paras` (the hunt sends its helicopters and its paramotor riflemen). They are the server's cvars, shown in its server info, and `sim::apply_hunt` hands them to a run's wanted level and army before its first step.
