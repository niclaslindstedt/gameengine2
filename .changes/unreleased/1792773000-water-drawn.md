title: The water's surface drawn
---

`render/water.hpp` draws any body of water from `engine/water` on the GPU: rings of quads round the eye, the simulation's own waves with finer detail waves past them, Fresnel between screen-space reflections of the scene (the sky behind them) and the water seen into through its own colour, and the sun's glitter from every slope too small to draw, so far water never shimmers. Fall Line's frame draws it (`add_water`). The `gpu-water` lab (a lake, the sea in three winds, sunset, every look, a fly-out) and the `lake` lab (water in Fall Line's own picture).
