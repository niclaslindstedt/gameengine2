type: Added
title: Grass that sways in the wind
---

`ge::grass` (`engine/grass`, published as `ge::grass`) grows grass blade by blade: six presets (lawn, meadow, long, tussock, steppe, reeds) and recipes that override them (`meadow height=0.6 density=300`), covers of triangles from a heightfield's region or a mesh's up-facing floors, every blade scattered off its triangle's own seed with clumps, and a blade's curve in the engine's wind (bent by the kind's give, swaying at its own frequency, its length kept), with gusts carried downwind as fronts. `engine/render/grass.hpp` grows the same blades on the GPU every frame, thins them with distance without popping, and draws them as strips bent in the wind and lit through from behind. The `grass` and `gpu-grass` labs picture every kind, the wind and the reach.
