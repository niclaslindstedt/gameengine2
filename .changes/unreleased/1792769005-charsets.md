type: Added
title: Ten bitmap fonts, noise layers and text looks (engine/charset)
---

`engine/charset` (`ge::charset`) gives every game ten monospaced ASCII fonts for messages, menus, the console and the HUD: terminal, matrix, segment, mono, typewriter, square, stencil, gothic, rounded and marker. Each is committed as a coverage atlas at 256, 512 and 1024 px: a 16 x 8 grid of 1:2 cells, ASCII 32..126, UI symbols in the control codes (cursors, arrows, checkboxes, a slider, box drawing, shades) and a missing glyph. It also gives eight tileable noise layers (grain, grunge, scratches, scanlines, rust, cracks, halftone, brushed) and ten looks built from them (plain, shadow, outlined, rusted, phosphor, neon, worn, chrome, carved, amber), with a CPU compositor and text layout (`paint_text`). `engine/lab` gains a deterministic deflater and `encode_png_grey`. New labs: `charsets` and `charset-looks`. See `docs/charsets.md`.
