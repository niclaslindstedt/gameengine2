title: The view under water
---

`render/underwater.hpp` redraws the picture when the camera is under a body of water: the scene fading into the water's own colour by its visibility, red first; the bed lit through the water above it; the bare eye's blur by distance; Snell's window looking up, with the water mirrored past it; the eye adapting to the dark. Presets for open eyes, a mask, murky water and a classic stylised tint and warp. Fall Line's frame draws it. The `gpu-underwater` lab, and views under a tarn in the `lake` lab.
