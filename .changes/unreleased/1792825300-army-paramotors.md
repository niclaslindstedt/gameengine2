title: Fall Line's army flies paramotor riflemen
---

From four stars Fall Line's army sends riflemen on paramotors: the wanted level's roster calls two rigs at four stars and four at five, each flown in already aloft from the map's edge on the free ride's own wing and engine, steered by the paramotor's bot to a point ahead round a circle over the hunted skier (wider and higher round where he was last seen), kept off the other rigs and the helicopters, never asked for less than 100 m over the snow and turning only gently when under its floor. Each rifleman fires bursts under the hunt's rules of fire whenever he has the skier in sight and in reach, keeps the stars up as one of the hunt's pursuers, and flies home to the map's edge once the hunt is over; a rig flown into the snow or a crown is let go and lost. A rig can be put in flight with `paramotor::aloft_para`, and the new `army-para` lab tables and pictures the rigs on seed 38.
