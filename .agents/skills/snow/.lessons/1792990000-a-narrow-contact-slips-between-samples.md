---
title: A contact narrower than a cell slips between the samples; widen it and keep its pressure
date: 2026-10-23
scope: engine/snow/press
concepts: [press, cell, narrow, ski, berm, mass, spilled]
---

A carving ski is 7 cm wide on a 10 cm cell, so its outline covered no sample on most steps and
its berm ring found none either: the press packed nothing and the thrown snow was counted as
spilled. `press` widens an outline to 0.75 of a cell (the load spread over the widened area, so
the pressure stays the contact's own) and puts the berm ring at least 1.5 cells out. A new
shape or a finer cell must be checked against the same failure: `snow-press` fails on any
spilled mass inside the region.
