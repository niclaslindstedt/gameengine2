Tests: `tests/services_catalog_test.cpp`, `tests/services_sim_store_test.cpp`, `tests/services_offline_test.cpp`.

Lab: `services` (four players on one script; every event, the journal, each answer's wait). At 50 ms of latency and 30 ms of jitter every answer arrived within 83 ms (five 60 Hz frames), all 71 events the script is owed, three of them refused as staged (a rate limit, a cut connection).
