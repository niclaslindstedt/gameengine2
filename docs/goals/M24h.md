(`Water::eye_under`, found by `prepare_water`), one full-screen pass redraws the frame from the
water's copies as seen through that water: the scene fading by the body's beam attenuation into

eye's blur by distance, Snell's window and the mirror past it, the eye's adaptation to the dark,
a wobble and a flat tint. Presets: `eyes-open`, `mask`, `murky`, `classic`. Fall Line's frame
runs it (`GameFrame::under`; the row is empty in the air).

degrees against 48.6, a dive through the surface) and the `lake` lab's views under a tarn, both
in `make labs-smoke`. Pictures: the Water labs page.
