Status: done

Landed `engine/water` (`ge::water`, published as `ge::water`):

- `spectrum.hpp`: the fetch-limited growth laws capped at the developed sea, the JONSWAP spectrum
  normalised to the asked height, the depth factor, the directional spread and the dispersion
  relation.
- `waves.hpp`: the simulation tier's components (24 by default, laid by equal weight of energy
  and slope, directions through the spread by stratified, shuffled quantiles, phases off
  `core::Rng`), the second-order surface with exact slopes and orbital velocity, and the three
  breaking shares (crest, shoal, whitecap).
- `fetch.hpp`: the effective fetch by rays upwind over a body's outline, baked on a grid.
- `world.hpp`: `World` and its four kinds of body (`add_lake`, `add_ocean`, `add_river`,
  `add_volume`), each one call, and the one `sample` query with its batch form: the surface,
  normal, water velocity (waves and a river's current), depth, bed, density and breaking.

Evidence: `tests/water_spectrum_test.cpp`, `water_waves_test.cpp` (a 10 000 s series measures
the laid Hs within 5 % and the mean period within 12 %), `water_fetch_test.cpp` and
`water_world_test.cpp` (a river's profile carries its discharge to 0.25 %), with goldens
`tests/golden/water_waves.txt` and `water_world.txt`; the `waves` and `fetch` labs in
`make labs-smoke`.

Level of detail: the waves are a pure function evaluated only where asked, so their simulation
tier costs nothing away from the actors; the actor-measured tiers that do cost (sleeping floats,
coarsened and sleeping flow regions) land with M24c and M24f, the picture's with M24g and M24k.
