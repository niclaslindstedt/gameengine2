Status: done

`engine/render/grass.hpp` with `grass.wgsl`, `grass_scatter.wgsl` and `wgsl/grass_common.wgsl`:
`grass.scatter` grows a cover's blades on the GPU each frame exactly as engine/grass does (one
workgroup a triangle, culled by the frustum and the reach, kept by rank against the distance,
faded instead of popped, a fine and a coarse list into indirect draws), and `grass` draws them as
triangle strips bent in the wind grid round the eye, lit two-sided with translucency. Measured on
the software adapter: the GPU's blades match the CPU's (the lean within 2e-3 rad, at least 99.8 %
of them), the shapes within 2 mm, the air within 1e-3 m/s (`tests/render_grass_test.cpp`); the
`gpu-grass` lab's eight views (about 130,000 blades each) in about 11 s at 480 px.
