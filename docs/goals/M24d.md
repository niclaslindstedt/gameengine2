Status: done

Landed `engine/water/swim.hpp`: the immersion level (0 to 3, from the water at the feet, the
waist and the eyes, all through `sample`), the kinematic regime (a first-person feel in the
game's own units: the wish by the input and the view's pitch, friction by the level, an
acceleration up to the wish, the slow sink with no input, no gravity), the body regime (an
upright column of the swimmer's height: gravity on him and his gear, buoyancy on his body, his
lungs and each garment's own material and trapped air, quadratic drag raised as his clothes
soak, the stroke cut by the soak, the cold shock, swim failure and spent stamina), the breath,
the cold (`failure_time`), the water jump over a lip the game reports through a callback, and
splash, stroke and gasp events. `water::default_swimmer()` is a person in a swimsuit;
`plugins/skier/swim.hpp`'s `dressed_skier()` is the skier in his jacket, trousers, boots,
helmet, gloves and base layers.

Evidence: `tests/water_swim_test.cpp` (the three heights; the kinematic regime settling at
accelerate / (friction x level) of its wish, half the input giving half; the water jump; a
swimmer who swims up keeps his eyes out and one lying still drowns and sinks; the breath held
less than half as long in 2 degree water; swim failure at `failure_time` and not before, never
in warm water; the events; a golden of both regimes over a minute,
`tests/golden/water_swim.txt`) and `tests/skier_swim_test.cpp` (more than 5 kg soaked within two
minutes, the eyes under between one and four minutes after the plunge settles, metres down and
drowning by eight; swimming up holds his eyes out at least a minute longer). The `swim` and
`skier-swim` labs in `make labs-smoke`.

Measured (`skier-swim`, 1 degree water): the skier floats on about 23 kg of trapped air, soaks
9.1 kg, has his eyes under for good at 159 s lying still and 334 s swimming up, and is out of
breath at 218 s and 352 s; a swimmer in a swimsuit lying still is under for good at 15 s.

Level of detail: a swimmer is an actor; nothing in the swim is tiered.
