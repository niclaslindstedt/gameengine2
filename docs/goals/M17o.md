Status: done

Smoothing is in `engine/net/replicate` (`spring`, `cubic`, `extrapolate_s`, all on by default):

- **Corrections.** A correction's error is drawn out through a critically damped spring with a
  half-life of 1/15 s. The spring carries its own velocity, so the drawn path bends where a decay
  kinked it. A correction past `snap_distance` (off by default) is shown at once.
- **The others between snapshots.** They follow a cubic Hermite curve whose slopes come from the
  snapshots either side, so the path has no corner at each snapshot.
- **The others past the newest snapshot.** They are carried on at their last velocity for up to
  0.2 s, then held. When the late snapshot arrives they blend back over 0.13 s. A field marked
  `extrapolate = false` (a count, a flag) is held at once. The row asked for the game's own step
  with the last input held, but a client has the others' states and not their inputs, so the
  velocity between the last two snapshots stands in. M17p's predicted world is where others'
  inputs reach the client.
- **Any tick rate.** Every duration in `ReplicateConfig` (the slack window, the starve hold, the
  spring, the extrapolation cap and blend) is now in seconds and turned into ticks at
  `tick_hz`. `engine/net/net_cvars.hpp` binds `sv_fps` (latched, 10 to 240 Hz) and the rate and
  smoothing cvars to the config.

Measured by the `net-predict` lab, now run at 72 Hz (a shooter's) and 120 Hz (Fall Line's) in
three modes, and held by `tests/net_predict_test.cpp` and `tests/net_cvars_test.cpp`:

- **The others' jerk.** At 72 Hz the 99th percentile falls against time sync alone: from 12,900
  m/s³ to 4,600 on `broadband`, from 25,300 to 7,600 on `wifi`, and from 83,400 to 59,700 on
  `awful`.
- **The own path's jerk.** Leaving out the ticks where the client's own input changed, it falls
  from 26,200 m/s³ to 6,100 on `bad` and from 37,900 to 7,600 on `awful`. At 120 Hz it falls from
  43,900 to 3,900 and from 85,600 to 9,200.
- **Late snapshots.** The others drawn past the newest snapshot land a mean 0.28 m from the
  server's truth when held and 0.01 m when carried on (`bad`, 72 Hz). The lab charts the error by
  how late.
- **No snaps.** No correction snaps with the distance off. The tests hold that a correction past
  the distance is shown at once.
- **72 Hz.** On `lan` an input reaches the server's step in 14 ms and the others are drawn 31 ms
  behind. At 120 Hz the figures are 8 ms and 19 ms.

Not taken: the view delay is unchanged by smoothing. A lower delay quantile, with the
extrapolation covering the rest, is a tuning left for M17p's measurements.
