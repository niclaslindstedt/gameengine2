Not started: Shooter's Edge starts once Fall Line is on the engine (`docs/shooters-edge.md`).

Water movement is not written here: the engine's swimmer (`engine/water/swim`, M24d) has a
kinematic regime built for this game's feel, and `plugins/shooter` holds the classic preset
(its numbers) and maps a brush world's water, slime and lava contents onto `engine/water`
volumes. The `pmove` lab's water entry and swim cases hold the preset.

The numbers the engine's own tests and `swim` lab use for a first-person preset (units of 32 a
metre: run 320, the swim's share 0.7, accelerate 10, friction 4 a level, idle sink 60, the water
jump 225 up and 50 forward for 2 s under a gravity of 800) are a starting point; the preset this
game ships is measured against a recording in `se-parity`.
