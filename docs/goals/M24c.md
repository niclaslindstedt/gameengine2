Status: done

Landed `engine/water/float.hpp`: probes (spheres and slabs) fixed in a body's frame, each read
through the one `sample` query, giving buoyancy by the submerged share, linear and quadratic
drag against the water's own velocity, the entry slam fading out by half wet, the added mass,
a planing plate's lift and skin friction, and splash events where a probe wets or leaves the
water. `float_push` returns the force and torque for a caller that integrates its own body;
`step_floater` integrates a crate, a log or a buoy itself, with the added mass's Lagrangian term
so wetting makes no energy.

Evidence: `tests/water_float_test.cpp` (boxes settle within 5 mm of their density's draught,
heave periods within 3 % of theory with and without added mass, no energy gained undamped, a
loss every damped swing, the slam and the splash, a sled that skims at speed and sinks when
slow, a buoy whose heave matches the surface's within 20 %) and its golden
`tests/golden/water_float.txt`; the `float` lab in `make labs-smoke` (draughts within 0.2 mm,
heave periods within 0.03 %).

Level of detail: floats near an actor run every step; sleeping a float with no actor near is
left to M24k, which lays out the tiers a game sets.
