Status: done

The texture generator is `engine/texgen` (design: `docs/textures.md`, skill: `textures`).

- The `.tex` recipe and its parser. Every mistake is a diagnostic with its line and the fix.
  Texture references (`name seed=<n> <param>=<v>`) and params resolve and clamp, and each
  layer's seed is mixed from the recipe's, the reference's and the layer's place
  (`texgen_recipe_test`).
- Eight tileable noise kinds (value, perlin, cells, edges, voronoi, ridged, turbulence, white)
  with octaves, gain and domain warp. Each is periodic at the tile's edge, stays in 0..1 and
  holds its bits (`texgen_noise_test`, `tests/golden/texgen_noise.txt`, `texgen-noise`).
- Eighteen layer kinds in one table: base (fill, noise, stripes), pattern (bricks, tiles,
  planks, panels) and life (cracks, scratches, dirt, dust, grime, wear, rust, streaks, moss,
  snow, glow). `ge texture kinds` prints the table.
- The bake to albedo, normal, ORM, glow and height. Every library recipe gives the same bytes
  in debug and release, and holds its digest at 64 px (`tests/golden/texgen_bake.txt`).
- The disk cache is keyed by the recipe's canonical text, the seed and the params. A variant
  is baked once and then reused (`texgen_bake_test`, `cli_texture_test`).
- A library of twelve recipes: brick wall, cave rock, concrete, wood planks, metal panels,
  rusted metal, tech panel (lit lines), lava rock (glowing cracks), snowy stone, flagstones,
  bathroom tiles and hazard stripes. All twelve tile with a seam score of at most 0.9
  (`texgen-specimens`).
- `ge texture kinds`, `check` and `bake`, and the `textures` consumer skill.
- Cost at 512 px on the 4-core container, release build: 55 to 165 ms a recipe, with the
  layer kinds' costs in `texgen-cost`.
