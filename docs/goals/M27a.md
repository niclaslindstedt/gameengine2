Status: done

`engine/charset` (`ge_charset`, `docs/charsets.md`): ten monospaced bitmap fonts, eight noise
layers and ten looks.

- The atlas: 128 codes (ASCII 32..126, UI symbols in 1..31, a missing glyph at 127) on a
  16 x 8 grid of 1:2 cells, one channel of coverage, committed at 256, 512 and 1024 px
  (`engine/charset/atlases/`, 30 files, 0.9 MB).
- Seven drawn fonts (mono, typewriter, square, stencil, gothic, rounded, marker) come from one
  set of skeletons, each under its own pen: round, square or a broad nib, with slab serifs,
  stencil breaks, lozenge feet, a slant, a hand's wobble or rough edges. They are rasterized
  analytically with no supersampling. Three more are built differently: terminal (8 x 16 pixel
  art), matrix (5 x 9 lamps) and segment (a 16-segment display).
- Eight tileable layers (grain, grunge, scratches, scanlines, rust, cracks, halftone, brushed)
  are committed at 256 and 512 px (`engine/charset/layers/`). Ten looks (plain, shadow,
  outlined, rusted, phosphor, neon, worn, chrome, carved, amber) are composed on the CPU off an
  exact distance field.
- `engine/lab/deflate.hpp`: a deterministic deflater, so a committed PNG is the same bytes on
  every platform (`encode_png_grey`).
