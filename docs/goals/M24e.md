Status: done

Landed `engine/water/optics.hpp`: the colour of water from what is in it, computed spectrally
every 10 nm from 400 to 700 nm. Pure water's measured absorption and its scattering, dissolved
organic matter (exponential in the wavelength), phytoplankton (a typical absorption shape scaled
by chlorophyll, and its scattering), mineral sediment and bubbles sum to the absorption, the
scattering and the backscattering; the diffuse attenuation follows from them; a semi-analytic
shallow-water model gives the water-leaving colour over the bed at any depth; under water a view
looks through the water's own veil, a target fading as exp(-c d), and a black target is lost at
4.8 / c. Spectra become linear sRGB through the CIE 1931 observer under daylight. `water::Optics`
is what the renderer reads (three bands, the deep colour, the veil, the bed, the emission, the
visibility). Eleven named looks (`water::looks()`), each a row of constituents with the band of
visibility such water has in the field; a body's `Stuff::look` names one, or `Stuff::water`
states a game's own, and `Body::optics` is derived once when the body is laid (an unknown name
warns and falls back to nordic-clear).

Evidence: `tests/water_optics_test.cpp` (white stays white; pure water absorbs the red and is
blue; the humus amber, the plankton green, the flour turquoise, the mud brown; the shallows fade
from the bed to the deep monotonically; daylight dims red first; a target fades into the veil;
every look found and inside its field band; a body takes its look's optics; a golden of every
look, `tests/golden/water_optics.txt`). The `water-colour` lab in `make labs-smoke`.

Measured visibility: nordic-brown 1.6 m, nordic-clear 8.6, baltic 2.6, adriatic 23, tropical
shallows 22, glacial 2.8, algal pond 0.45, muddy river 0.12, pool 41.
