Status: in progress

Built (PR #145): `rooms::clone_map` clones a version 29 or BSP2 map into a room level. The tree's solid, liquid and sky cells become brushes, each visible face takes its surface's texture and recovered projection, brush models become groups (a trigger's as trigger volumes), and the entities come across with `origin` in metres and `angle` as `heading`. Textures come only from an agent-approved texture map (`parse_texture_map`), or placeholders on request, and are never copied. The `--smooth` option shades faceted arches round. The `clone` lab draws each map beside its original from the same eyes and stands a body at every start. `tests/rooms_clone_test.cpp` holds the fixture's clone golden. The shareware maps clone and a body stands at their starts (numbers in the PR).
