Status: done

Landed `engine/water/flow.hpp`: water simulated on a grid over a bed. A finite-volume scheme
(HLL fluxes over the hydrostatic reconstruction, so still water over any bed stays still and a
front runs onto dry ground at its own speed), Manning's friction, CFL sub-steps. A cell's
water is a whole count of quanta (1e-9 m^3), so no pass makes or loses any; the passes run on
bands of rows and give the same bits on one thread and eight. Springs (`Source`), drains
emptying by Torricelli's law (`Drain`), edges left open (`FlowSpec::open`); a wall is bed
raised, broken by lowering it (`set_bed`). A lip whose bed drops more than `fall_drop` sends its
water off as a sheet on a ballistic path that lands after its time in the air (`Flow::falls`
for the curtain). `world.hpp`'s `add_flow` lays a region as a body, `step_flows` steps them all,
and `sample` reads its surface, normal and current where it is wet.

Its level of detail: `set_tier` merges the cells 2x2 (half the rate) or 4x4 (a quarter),
keeping each coarse face's crest so thin walls hold, and levels the water back over the fine
beds on the way down, to the quantum; `tier_for` picks the tier from the game's actors with
hysteresis; a far, settled region sleeps until a nearer tier, a source, a drain or a breach
wakes it.

Evidence: `tests/water_flow_test.cpp` (still water stays still; Ritter's dam break; Torricelli
within 1%; a breach as a weir, power 1.3 to 1.7; the books to the quantum; a waterfall's sheet
landing beyond its lip; one thread against eight; the tiers hold the water and follow the
actors; a far region sleeps and wakes; `sample` reads a region; a breach's golden,
`tests/golden/water_flow.txt`). The `flow` and `water-lod` labs in `make labs-smoke`; the
`water-cost` bench.

Measured: Ritter 0.015 m rms at 5 s, 0.005 at 20 s; the weir's power 1.49 and coefficient 0.78;
a 2 m^3/s river over a 6 m cliff carried whole by its sheets, landing 1.75 m out; a flood
changed tier six times holds its water to the quantum, its surface moving a median of 1 to 3 cm.

Not yet: waking a lake or a volume into a region (it lands with the one call, M24k). The
step's cost in a release build, and a thread pool for `core::parallel_bands` (it starts its
threads every pass, so eight threads are slower than one on a small region in `water-cost`),
are owed to the perf ledger.
