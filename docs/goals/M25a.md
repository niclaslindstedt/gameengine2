Status: done

Built:

- `engine/save/xxh64.hpp`: the XXH64 checksum, held to the algorithm's published values.
- `engine/save/io.hpp`: the state described once (`describe(io, T&)`), walked by the `Writer`
  and the `Reader`; numbers, enums, bools, strings, vectors, arrays, optionals, pairs and
  `core::Rng`; bulk copies of numbers and `GE_SAVE_PLAIN` types (`plain_problem` checks one);
  `since`, `until` and `io.version()`; a reader that never reads past its bytes and keeps its
  first problem with the byte.
- `engine/save/file.hpp`: the file (64-byte header, the meta chunk first, tagged and versioned
  chunks on 8-byte boundaries with zero padding, the directory and its checksum), `FileWriter`
  into a reused buffer, `FileReader` that checks every byte, `peek` for a listing.
- `ge save inspect`; the `save` skill and the `saves` consumer skill.

Measured by the `save-file` lab (release, the container): a 4.1 MB state of 64 racers of 2,000
points round-trips to the byte; written in 0.92 ms (4.5 GB/s), opened with every checksum
checked in 0.50 ms (8.3 GB/s), opened and read back into the state in 0.95 ms (4.4 GB/s); the
header and meta peeked in 0.12 us. On a 1,384-byte file every one of the 1,384 cuts and 1,384
flipped bytes is refused. An older chunk reads its defaults; a newer one is refused naming both
versions.
