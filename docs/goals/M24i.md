Status: done

Landed `engine/water/wake.hpp`: wakes as deep-water impulse rings, a moving hull's trail of them
(`wake_trail`) and a splash's (`float.hpp`'s `splash_ring`), summed by `sample` into a lake's or a
sea's surface, so a float rides another's wake. Their sum opens at Kelvin's angle by itself:
measured 19.4 degrees for a 4 m hull at 3 m/s, narrowing at higher Froude numbers as real wakes
do. Drawn by the water pass with the waves. `engine/render/water_stream.hpp` and its shader: rivers
as ribbons, flow regions as their wet cells, every waterfall's sheet as a white streaked curtain
along its path, ripples carried by a two-phase flow map, lace foam where the water is fast,
supercritical or landing; the mist and spray (`waterfall_spray`, `splash_spray`) into the
particle pool. The still and moving water share `render/wgsl/water_light.wgsl`.

Evidence: the `float` lab's wake plan and its angle table at 3–6 m/s; `gpu-water`'s `wake`,
`rings` (with a splash's spray), `river` and `waterfall` views; `tests/water_wake_test.cpp`
(group speed, Kelvin's angle within 2 degrees, the rings in `sample`, a buoy rocked, a golden)
and `tests/render_water_stream_test.cpp`. Pictures: the Water labs page.

Not yet: the wake's rings in a channel or a flow region (they ride only on lakes and seas); the
spray lit by the water's own optics; a river's banks feathered as a lake's shore is.

Next: M24j (water's sound), then M24k (water in one call) and M24l (Fall Line's water); M24g2
and M24h2 stay open.
