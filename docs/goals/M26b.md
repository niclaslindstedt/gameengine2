The camera's settings are cvars (`engine/camera/cvars.hpp`, `docs/camera.md` § Settings and
cvars): `cl_fov`, `cl_viewheight`, `cl_bob`, `cl_bobcycle`, `cl_bobup`, `cl_rollangle`,
`cl_rollspeed`, `v_kicktime`, `v_kickpitch`, `v_kickroll`, `v_idlescale`, `cl_landdip`,
`cl_chasedist`, `cl_chaseheight`, `cl_fovspeed`, `cl_camblend`, `cl_motion`, `cl_shake`, and
`cl_camera` when a game asks for it. A game registers them with its defaults and its world unit
(`CameraDefaults`); they are bound to a `CameraCvars` in their own units and written over the
ladder's rows in metres and radians once a frame (`apply_camera_cvars`), a 0 length or lens
leaving the row its own.

Fall Line registers them (at the engine's defaults, metres; its view stays `cl_view`) and the
window applies them to every ladder it holds, the skis' and the machines'; `ge cvar ls cl_` lists
them. `tests/camera_cvars_test.cpp` sets every one from one cfg line in a world of inches and
reads the rows back; at `cl_motion 0` no rig of the reference ladder rolls the horizon through
walk, strafe, carve or fire. `camera-rigs --motion=0` runs every scenario at comfort 0 and fails a
roll (none: every rig held level); `camera-shake` already holds comfort 0 still. The default
`camera-rigs` digest did not move.
