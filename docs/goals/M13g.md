`plugins/mountain/village` lays the village's streets as a pure function of the map
(`plan_streets`, off hashes of the seed, never a stream; no map digest moves): a loop of the main
street, the back street and the cross streets below the hub, one or two roads out off the map's
edge, the square below the main lift and the day car park with its aisles. Every street, to the
back of its sidewalks, keeps off the runs, the lifts' lines, stations and queue lanes, the hub and
its tunnels, the gates, the start, the finish, the kickers and the pads the game names, and climbs
no more than 9 % along and 7 % across. `bake_ground` paints it onto a metre grid (road, street,
felled, garden) and `felled_trees` reads the trees it takes.
