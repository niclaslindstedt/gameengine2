Status: done

Landed `engine/render/water.hpp` and its shaders: the water's surface drawn on the GPU. Two
passes after the opaque scene and the sky: `water.copy` keeps the frame's colour and depth, and
`water` draws every body over them. The geometry is nested squares of 64 quads round the eye,
snapped and morphed like the terrain's rings, clamped into the body's box, the last ring
reaching the horizon. The waves are the simulation's own components in the same formula, plus 32
detail components from the same spectrum down to 4 cm; a vertex and a pixel each keep only the
waves they can resolve, and the slopes they drop become the glitter's roughness, so far water
glints as a path and never shimmers. Fresnel between the mirror (screen-space reflections of the
opaque scene, the sky's model behind them) and the water seen into (the scene behind, bent and
dimmed by the body's optics, over the water's own colour).

Fall Line's frame now owns the passes (`add_water`, `set_water`; both rows run empty with no
water), and the `lake` lab digs a tarn into a seed's map to draw it in the game's own picture.

Evidence: `tests/render_water_test.cpp` (the detail band and the levels); the `gpu-water` lab
(the lake calm and in a wind, every look, the sea in three winds, sunset, a fly-out from 2 m to
2 km, the drawn crest read back at 0.8133 m against the simulation's 0.8139 m) and the `lake` lab
(shore, aerial, into the sun, a fresh wind, evening), both in `make labs-smoke`. Pictures: the
Water labs page.
