Status: done

Built (PR "capture: video with sound, the engine's own file"):

- `engine/capture` (compiled into `ge_play`): `ebml` and `matroska` (the muxer: clusters cut at
  keyframes, the seek head, cues, duration and segment size patched on close, a crash leaving
  every whole cluster readable), `mkv_read` (the reader the tests and labs use), `yuv` (RGBA to
  I420 and back, BT.709 limited range in integers), `av_export` (the A/V clock: the synth
  rendered to each step's sample before the run's sound hears it), `sync_fixture` (a click and a
  flash on one step).
- `play/game.hpp`: the run's sound (`Sound`, `SoundSpec`, `GE_SOUND`); `record --format=mkv`
  (`ge record`) writes the pictures with the sound, no ffmpeg; `--sound=0` leaves it out.
- Fall Line's sound registered (`games/fall_line/sound/play_sound.hpp`).

Measured by the `capture` lab (debug build, 30 s at 24, 25, 30 and 60 fps): every click's sound
on its step's sample (0.02 ms off), the first bright picture after it within a frame, ffmpeg
decoding every picture; a 720p frame costs about 26 ms to convert and mux, 2.5 GB a minute
uncompressed. The `capture-run` lab exports seed 38's race with its sound and reads it back.
